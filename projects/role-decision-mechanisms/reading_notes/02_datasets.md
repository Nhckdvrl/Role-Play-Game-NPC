## Other benchmark facts worth remembering
- **SimpleToM** (2410.13648): ~1,147 two-sentence stories × 3 question types (mental state / behaviour prediction / behaviour judgment). Explicit ToM high, behaviour prediction much lower, judgment lower still; a *question-specific mental-state reminder* (insert the model's own answer to the awareness question) closes most of the gap; CoT* that tells the model to consider mental states reaches 87–93%. This is Knowing vs Predicting in third person, already well documented.
- **T4D** (2310.03051): "If you were there, what would you do?" version of ToMi; GPT-4 50% vs humans 90% while ToMi ≈ 93%. An early first-person "Enacting" vs "Knowing" gap.
- **Belief–behaviour in role-play agents** (2507.02197, Trust Game): the model's elicited beliefs about how a persona will act (≈ Predicting) predict its own simulated behaviour (≈ Enacting) poorly; context during elicitation doesn't help; imposed priors make it worse; forecasts degrade over longer horizons.
- **CausalToM** (in Lookbacks, 2505.14685): templated two-character stories with counterfactual pairs built for interchange interventions, including visibility sentences. Directly reusable methodology for the access-flag question.
